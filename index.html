My Routine & Habits

A typical week in my busy life.

    I wake up very early to get ready and go to the high school.

  • 03:00 PM

    I finish my ASIR classes and go back home to eat quickly.

  • 04:00 PM

    I start working. My job takes most of my afternoon and evening.

Saturdays and Sundays are my days to disconnect. I wake up late because I am tired from the week. I spend the whole day with my girlfriend, my stepson, or riding my motorcycle on the road with my friends. I try not to think about work or studies.

My Habits

  • Start Eating healthier: I want to start a better diet because I usually eat fast due to my schedule.
  • Stop Sleeping late: I want to stop sleeping only 5 hours because I feel very tired during the day.
  • Keep Riding bikes: I want to keep going to the circuit because it helps me relieve stress.

4 Tips to Improve a Routine

Sleep at least 7-8 hours

It is medically proven to be good for your brain, your body, and your energy levels for the next day.

Use a Calendar App

When you work and study at the same time, using Google Calendar is very useful to organize your free time.

Stay Hydrated

Drinking a lot of water is very healthy, especially when you have long days or do physical activities like riding.

Exercise regularly

Riding a motorcycle at a professional circuit requires a lot of physical form, so going to the gym is a great idea.

Professional Goals

My Experience with English

My honest opinion about English is that it is absolutely necessary today. We live in a globalized world.

For my professional career, it is essential. In the IT sector (networks, servers, programming), 90% of the documentation, manuals, and software are written in English. If I want to be a good Systems Administrator and work remotely for big companies, I need to improve my English skills. It is also very helpful when I travel or when I watch motorcycle races from around the world.
